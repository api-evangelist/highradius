# HighRadius

> HighRadius is an AI-powered autonomous finance platform for the Office of the CFO, automating Order-to-Cash (O2C), Record-to-Report (R2R), Accounts Payable (AP), and Treasury with a network of orchestrated AI agents. It serves 1,500+ enterprises with pre-built integrations to 50+ ERPs including SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, and NetSuite.

Note: HighRadius does not publish a public developer portal or OpenAPI specification. Integration is delivered through pre-built ERP connectors and partner/professional-services engagements. The links below are the public surfaces the API Evangelist network has verified.
